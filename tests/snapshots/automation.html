Rules to lock before connecting Squarespace

Mailing cadence

Everletter has two batch dates per month: the 1st and the 15th.

3-day cutoff

A new subscription can join a batch when the signup/payment is at least roughly 3 days before the 1st or 15th. Otherwise it rolls to the next batch.

Spreadsheet bridge

Until Squarespace sync is live, import the updated Everletter Mailing Schedule through the Import Sheet screen.
